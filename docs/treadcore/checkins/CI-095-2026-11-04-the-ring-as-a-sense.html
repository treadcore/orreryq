Abstract

A sense of the nearest live pillbox's ring was added to the nets' senses and proved harmless to every shipped net. Nets imitating the rules with it did no better on the two ring gauntlets than nets without it, and worse on G7. By the rule written before the run it is not shipped; the code is parked in a stash.

I. Intent

Paul: "perhaps the sensor will indicate a ring, as it would indicate and identify the foe."

III. Method

The gauntlet for the rules and every shipped net, with the block and at LR 145's commit without it. RECORD_COURSE on G7, G8, G18 and G19, 2,000 seeds from 9,100,000; the same file with the block zeroed; treadcore-bc with BC_DEVICE=flex, 30 epochs, batches of 512, rate 0.001; each net on the gauntlet's 200 seeds.

IV. Results

Identical to the seed for every shipped net. Imitation, five runs a side, passed of 200: G7 42.1% with the ring, 52.0% without; G8 85.2% and 83.3%; G18 100% and 100%; G19 40.7% and 44.5%, each run anywhere from 0 to 172.

V. Discussion

The nets' failure on G18 was a lack of experience: imitating the rules where the choice comes up teaches it without the new sense. G19 is the open one, and its spread is the training's, not the senses'.

VI. What is not built

Several training starts with the best kept, for G19.

VII. Paul's decisions

Whether to drop the parked block or keep it for a later trial.
