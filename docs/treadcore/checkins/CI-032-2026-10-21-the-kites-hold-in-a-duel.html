Abstract

Backlog 55. The kite spends 62% of its time holding past the target's reach (LR 65); the hypothesis was that a shorter wait scores more. The meter, split by whom, puts 92% of the kite's seconds in rallies. A scenario's Commander could not rally, so scenarios gained pings = true and an outscores expectation. In a new family, a duel in the open over 400 seeds, a Commander that rallies on a RuleBot outscores it in 63.0% of seeds with the kite and 51.5% without. Twelve settings of kite_spare, kite_in and kite_margin read within about an error of the default, except kite_in 10, which is worse. The hypothesis is not supported; the defaults stand; the family holds a floor of 0.55. All six commits before this one were pushed at Paul's word.

Index terms: kite, rally, duel, scenario families, tuning.
