Abstract

Every census since LR 11 played naval.map alone (CI-001 A8). The census now plays a list of maps, MAP=all being all eleven, and reads a difference on each beside the pooled one. Its first reading puts the dodge, on against off, over 176 seeds: +5.94 +/- 0.38 a seat, 15.6 errors (read first part-way, 123 seeds, +6.00 +/- 0.48). Ten maps of eleven are past two errors for the dodge, and none against it. The dodge holds by the rule set before the run. Naval.map is mid-table, so the one map did not overstate the part.

Index terms: brain census, maps, paired difference, standard error, dodge.

III. Methods

The census. scripts/census.sh: the fair census (brain_census), trial weights caps = baseline (every part on), baseline caps = baseline, -dodge; MAP=all, which is the five prior-art maps, the two world maps, industry, island, naval and gen. Seeds 1000 to 1175 (16 a map), 900 s, 3 respawns, four jobs of 44 seeds from a binary copied when the run began; ledger off.

The part-way reading. For each seed, all three of its matches played (each team in the trial seat once): the trial team's mean score a seat, averaged over the three matches, less the baseline teams' mean. Its mean and standard error over seeds, overall and by map; past two errors is outside the noise. The census weights by seat (teams of 6, 5 and 5). This reading weights by team line, so it may differ from pooled.txt in the second decimal. The meter's dodge counts were summed over every match played.

Ashore, the dodged shell hits 2.4% of the time with the dodge and 23.0% without. Afloat it hits 0.5% against 4.3%, but deaths after the shell fall less (9.0% against 12.2%). The full table is in LR 71.

V. Discussion

  1. The dodge holds everywhere. It is the one part so far past two errors on a single map. On eleven maps it is past fifteen errors.
  2. Where it pays least. It pays least on the island-chain maps, where more of the fight is afloat. This matches LR 29: afloat, the dodge changes little (backlog 31).
  3. The method. Ten maps cost no more than one at the same seeds. Promotions can now ask for more than one map.
