Abstract

LR 78's sequential census was not adopted because one early stop (LR 73's -column) was false and final. This check-in builds the form LR 78 left to Paul: an early stop is read again, sequentially, on fresh seeds, and counts only if the re-read agrees. LOOKS=8 make census follows the census as it plays, from its match lines, which match the census's own seed lists to 0.01 over 976 seeds. The two stages hold the false alarm rate to 2.8%, below the fixed census's 4.8%, and -column now reads as the fixed census does 81% of the time. They cost seeds, though: 106% of the fixed census over LR 78's 44 censuses, and less power at the effects the census mostly reads (z 2.5: 53% against 68%). No re-read size beats the fixed census on both counts. By the rule set first it is an option for large expected effects, and the fixed census stays the default. A live test on the Mac exercised both paths, the end and the early stop with its re-read.

Index terms: brain census, sequential testing, O'Brien-Fleming, confirmation, statistical power, false alarm rate.

III. Methods

Reading as it plays. sequential.py watch DIR BIN every 20 s: each job's match lines, a seed finished when its three plays' nine team lines are read; its difference the trial team's mean score a seat less the baseline's (fleet.rs brain_census). The whole rounds across jobs, interleaved by first seed, are the census so far. At each look (round(j N / K)) the reading and the bound go to sequential.txt; past the bound before the last look, exit 3. census.sh then marks the directory stopped, kills the jobs by their frozen binary's path, and plays a second stage into reread/, its run.env SEED0 + SEEDS. sequential.py verdict reads both.

The check of (a). Four finished censuses from the machine (LR 72's dodge on every map, LR 76 -posture and -dodge, the board's nofront): the rebuilt differences against each job's "a seed:" list.

(b) and (c). sequential.py twostage over LR 78's 44 censuses: the simulation (N = 200 a stage, unit spread, 4 000 censuses a row, expected z 0 to 10) and the replay (stage one on the census's own seeds in order; the re-read drawn from them with replacement, 200 times). The size table, a quarter, a half and the whole as the re-read, 3 000 censuses a cell.

The live test. On the Mac, LOOKS=4 SEEDS=32 SEED0=900 SECS=120 JOBS=8 make census, naval.map, first TRIAL with every capability off, then a rookie (skill = 0).

IV. Results

In LR 79: (a) 976 seeds, the largest difference 0.01; (b) false alarms 2.8%; (c) 44 verdicts of 44 as often as not, 106% of the seeds; the power and size tables; the live test.

V. Discussion

Confirmation is not free. An early stop is cheap only when the effect is large enough to stop at the first looks of both stages. For the mid-sized effects most of this brain's parts have, the first stage stops at look four to six and the re-read then costs a census more. Each re-read also has a chance to miss a real effect, which is where the power goes. LR 78's single stage saved seeds by trusting the stop; LR 79's two stages are honest but spend the saving. The fixed census, with its rule before the run, was already the efficient instrument for the close calls this tuning makes. The sequential census earns its place as a fast screen for a large effect.
