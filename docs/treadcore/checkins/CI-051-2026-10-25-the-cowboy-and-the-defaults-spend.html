Abstract

The brain had no weight for spending: shells went at its target tank, longshots every 30 s at structures only, one strike drone at a time. Four weights let it spend, and a cowboy profile at loud values was plain to see. It also won by a margin no change had come near: +11.54 a seat on its census, +11.62 +/- 0.45 read again over 400 seeds, every map above. Taken apart, the longshots carry the fighting and shelling pillboxes is mostly LR 84's reward for it. retreat_shells does nothing at the defaults, because the retreat weights act through w_smooth 0.05. By the rule the spending became the defaults. make families then caught the dodge families at 91-96.5%: a pillbox shot turned the hull into a shell's splash. A pillbox shot now waits under an incoming shell, and the defaults as changed read +11.64 +/- 0.43 against the old.

III. Methods

The weights in Tactics (from_weights); RuleBot's fight: the pillbox shot after the gun and strikes, the longshot's target, the strike cap. meter::spend on each think's commands. Censuses on the machine, every map, 900 s, 24 jobs: the cowboy (110 from 120000), the re-read (400 from 130000), the parts (110 from 140000), the pair (400 from 150000, BASE=steady.weights). scripts/traits.py; the old score as score less rewards. On the Mac: cargo test -p treadcore-brain --release, make families with SET for each part.

IV. Results

In LR 86: the cowboy +11.54 +/- 0.89; re-read +11.62 +/- 0.45; the parts (shell_boxes +7.19, longshots +5.59, strikes +1.46, retreat_shells 0); the families; the pair +11.64 +/- 0.43, +5.52 without the rewards.

V. Discussion

The brain carried drones and shells it did not use. Spending them is worth more than any weighting of the ladder so far. Part of the measured gain is the reward for pillbox damage that LR 84 added an hour before, but even on the old score the change is four times turtle's. The families earned their place again: a census cannot see a tank shooting itself into a splash on 4% of seeds. The retreat weights acting a twentieth of the way is a finding for every profile that set them.
