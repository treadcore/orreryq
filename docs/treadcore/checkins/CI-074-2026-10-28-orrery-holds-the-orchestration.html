Abstract

The fleet's orchestration moved from treadcore to orrery:

  • the plan (what runs where), the Training view's document, the lanes and the counting are now in orrery/tools/plans/;
  • the fleet follows origin/main: each machine fetches, fast-forwards its own checkout, and builds each commit once;
  • the search reads the fleet's history where weights.rs is unchanged: 1,153 censuses;
  • a sync on the keeper's clock gathers every machine's ledger: 3,622 censuses in 13.7 s;
  • Shutdown Now: each machine finishes, reports and powers off, and a report says who finished what. Tested on a scratch queue.

saskatchewan's converge run stopped after generation 64 and the machine joined the standing queue, grove. On the new commit, all 40 candidates hold every family floor.

III. Methods

  1. orrery tools/plans/treadcore.py. It starts from fleet-plan.py and adds:
    • sha(), prep_id() and cdir();
    • same_defaults() and ledger() (history);
    • sync(only).
  2. orrery tools/fleet-queue.py:
    • power_off() and shutdown_report();
    • the keeper's sync thread, and its SHUTDOWN branch;
    • the commands shutdown-now and sync;
    • status with shutdown and sync.
  3. orrery src/main.rs: --queue. src/training.rs: the Sync now and Shut the fleet down buttons, and the sync and shutdown lines.
  4. The Shutdown test. A scratch queue: {here: "-"} and {boburner, poweroff: "true"}, six sleep 8 jobs, shutdown-now after 4 s.
  5. The grove queue. build/queue/grove/config.json and plan.json, as in orrery's guide section 1. ensure was run at 09:35.
  6. saskatchewan. A watcher waited for gen65/ to appear, then:
    • stopped converge, then the census by process name;
    • set saskatchewan's caps in grove to cpu.

Errors and limits:

  • No machine has a power-off rule yet, so a real shutdown would leave every machine running and report so.
  • Every machine's census binary has a different hash (each toolchain builds its own). That is why a census stays one machine's.

V. Discussion

The fleet can now be run without a shell:

  • start it, watch it, gather it, stop it, or shut it down, all from orrery;
  • train on the newest commit this Mac has pushed;
  • search from everything the fleet has measured under the same defaults.

What is left is the rest of the make targets (step 3), starting a run of each from orrery (step 4), promotion (step 5), and the Makefile itself (step 6).
