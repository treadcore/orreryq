Abstract

CI-015's reverse census read even where the forward read +2.31. This step took an absolute reading: every team on the new defaults against every team on the old (LR 27's null), on the same 16 seeds. Boats lost on the run for the beach nearly halve (165 to 89), but deaths in a dodge rise by about as many (354 to 434). The death rate is unchanged, the matches run 4.5% longer, and the team spends 13% more time alive. The deaths move ashore. This explains the asymmetry, and points the next step at the dodge's untuned numbers.

Index terms: absolute evaluation, attrition, displacement, defaults.

V. Discussion

  1. Saved tanks find the next danger. The landfall's parts move deaths from the water to the shore, and the rate stays where it was. A relative census shows that as a gain only while the opponents still lose their boats.
  2. Absolute readings belong in every promotion. Deaths, living time and match length sit beside the relative score from now on.
  3. The dodge is now half the deaths. It pays (CI-006), and its four numbers were first-guess constants. That is a tuning problem, and the tuner (backlog 23) has to be made honest, against the fair census and its error, before it is trusted with them.

Next: backlog 23 on the dodge's numbers.
