Abstract

dodge_own_route failed its first census on one map, big-lizard (−6.48 ± 1.56 over 10 seeds). A probe compared the own-route forecast with a fresh search at every ask: at sea they parted three times as often as ashore and turned the dodge's verdict three times as often. A test showed the own route is the truer forecast near guns: 0.00 tiles off against 7.85. Over 120 seeds on the Mac, big-lizard read −0.97 ± 0.66. The fix is the own route ashore only. Three reads on 110 fresh seeds each passed their rule: every map −0.18 ± 0.37, big-lizard +0.58 ± 0.81, and the generated maps +0.51 ± 0.43 (the one map below in the first read). make families held. The fix is on by default, and the run restarted on the new defaults. LR 94 is a primer on the day's implementation, with lessons. LR 95 designs a learned tank in Rust: senses as a compass of signal strengths, about 100 inputs, every action, rewards from driving toward a target upward, nine gated phases, evolution on the CPUs first.

Index terms: route forecast, multiple comparisons, sensor interface, evolution strategies, curriculum.

V. Discussion

The cost on big-lizard was mostly a crowded map's noise on ten seeds, over a smaller real effect that came from the sea. The fix removed the sea case and kept the saving. Two of the three "failures" in this story were the tail of many comparisons, which is why the rule now reads a failing map again before it decides. The learned tank is designed to be judged the same way: each phase gated by a family, the census the last word.
