Abstract

Each of the seven milestone nets was recorded at work on its defining test and drawn into a GIF in plain Rust (clip.rs, make clips): ten clips, one of them a duel and one a real match. A gauntlet of nine single-decision tests on bare ground (make gauntlet) read every net and the scripted brain on the same 200 seeds, with reaction times. It found two shortcuts the training maps hid: the map 7a net learned "few shells, go home", and the map 4 net learned the guarded scene, not the depot. It also found the scripted brain's blind spot: it fights inside a pillbox's ring. The match reading, now counting damage dealt, shows living and fighting pulling apart. On one M1 thread a decision costs 10.6 us, and a 16-seat match of nets runs at 197 times real time. The report is an HTML page with every clip.

Index terms: visualisation, behavioural testing, shortcut learning, reaction time, computation cost.

I. Introduction

A. Intent

Watch every generation, measure reactions and progress, write the report as a page, and build gauntlets of single decisions.

Can I get a short GIF of every generation of the tank AI battling. Show reaction measurements and progress on census. For each generation (or major milestone), show the capability of the tank AI during its action. Write a lab report in html. Describe out methods of using training based of recently designed brainz, which we are using as a basis to help develop the sandbox for testing and training. Descibe the quality of improvements. Describe the computation time of using the tank with its AI and only its AI. We want to watch, short clips of action. Please create a set gauntlets of test with maps for testing, and single decision with little distractions for testing.

III. Methods

make gauntlet (200 seeds a test from 9,500,000; every net widened to today's senses; the scripted brain is a Commander in the net's seat). make clips (a frame every 2 ticks, 15 s at most, the match clip 30 s). make train CHECK=net MATCH=40 for the untrained net and each milestone net. TIMING=1 for the cost. The page is built from build/train/gauntlet.tsv, the match readings, and the census reads of LR 97 to 100.

V. Discussion

The training maps say what a net can do in the situation it trained on. The gauntlet says what it learned. The two were not always the same, and the gauntlet is cheap enough (a minute for every net) to run after every training run from now on.
