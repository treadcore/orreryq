Abstract

On Paul's choice, landfall_memory 30, landfall_shy 1 and landfall_wait 10 are the defaults. As proof, the census was run in reverse: a team on the old defaults among two on the new. It read -0.08 ± 0.45 a seat, where the forward +2.31 ± 0.58 would predict about -2.3. The gain is relative to opponents that play the old way. What the weights do when every team has them is unmeasured: the census reads a team against the others. The defaults stay; backlog 38 takes an absolute reading.

Index terms: promotion, relative evaluation, interaction effects.

V. Discussion

A relative census answers "does a team with this beat teams without it?", and here the answer is yes. It does not answer "is the game better played when everyone has it?" For a default, which every bot takes, the second question is the one that matters, and needs a reading that is not relative. Every earlier promotion, the capped castle included, was read in one direction only.
