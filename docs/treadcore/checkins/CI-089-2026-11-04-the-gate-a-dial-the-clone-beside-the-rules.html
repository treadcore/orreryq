Abstract

The Gladiator read 2.5 a seat under the game's defaults, and the clone stage's first round could not tell a clone from the Scholar as a whole team of nets. The Duelist's gate is now a dial the census can read (duel_gate); the clone is judged on one seat beside the rules (SCHOLAR=file, bot_mix = one); and crucible's world islands spar against the game's styles. The gate swept at 0, 0.3 and 0.6 leaves the default at 0: 0.6 leans better by +1.61 ± 1.50, inside two errors.

I. Intent

orrery asked what should close the Gladiator's gap and how a clone should be judged. Paul chose, word for word: "Islands meet the game's styles", "The gate as a dial", and "One seat beside the rules".

V. Discussion

A champion kept to the fights it is surest of costs its team less: at 0.6 the Gladiator reads inside the noise of the rules. One error is not two, and 48 seeds are a tenth of a held-out read; the fleet's 400 will say whether 0.6 is the line.
