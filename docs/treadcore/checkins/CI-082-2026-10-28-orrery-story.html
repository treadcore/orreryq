Abstract

orrery's views are now five chapters in the order the work goes: Machines, Projects, Queue, Results, Watch. Each chapter's pages are metal keys in a steel column, with the chapter's sentence at its foot.

Training is split into Queue (what runs) and Results (what it found). A new Projects view shows treadcore and crucible side by side, and which machine runs which, from the queue's status and crucible's run files.
