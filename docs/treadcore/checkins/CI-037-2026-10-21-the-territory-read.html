Abstract

Goal 4's last open item: the team board built territory every second and nothing read where it was. LR 9's design gave it one job, Defend when there is threat in our territory. The board now keeps the held ground, the influence of the side's pillboxes and depots alone, thresholded at one work's value at 14 tiles. A hostile on it beyond the base's alarm is an incursion: it counts toward Defend at w_defend_ground (1), and the guard goes to the raided outlying work instead of rotating. In a family of a raid on an outlying pillbox while the team is away expanding, the Commander meets the raider in 400 of 400 seeds, none at 0. The census guard reads -0.36 +/- 0.27 a seat over 48 seeds, inside the noise; the read acts about 22 seconds a match. One older family loses 3.7 points and holds its floor.
