Abstract

Backlog 29 asked whether the census, with half the seat-time dead, hid the parts that pay late. Every capability off by default was measured alone, at unlimited respawns and at 3, under today's defaults, 12 seeds each. The base cycle and the escort read inside the noise both ways: not confirmed. Three of 24 readings fell outside two errors, where about one is expected by chance. Only smooth, negative in all four readings on record, stands as a finding. bridge_jobs (+1.68) and duck (-2.26) are leads for confirmation. On Paul's choice, smooth becomes a weight with an insignificant default.

Index terms: multiple comparisons, attrition, ablation, census design.

IV. Results

See LR 44's table. Outside two errors: smooth -3.32 ± 1.47 (unlimited), bridge_jobs +1.68 ± 0.70 (3), duck -2.26 ± 1.05 (3). Never acting: board alone, island_site.

V. Discussion

  1. The attrition hypothesis is not confirmed. Given the full 900 s without running out, the late parts still read nothing.
  2. Read a table of tests as a table. At two errors, one in twenty is outside by chance. Consistency across independent passes, or a confirmation on fresh seeds, separates a finding from a draw.
  3. Faster testing (Paul's question): noise costs more than CPU. Freeze the binary so work goes on during a census; measure each part on its own counts; test single behaviours in scenarios (backlog 24); more cores; a cheaper match (finding L2); sequential rules set in advance.
