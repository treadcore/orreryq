Abstract

Phase 0 of the learned tank is built, tested and read. The senses are 101 numbers: a compass of 8 sectors by 10 channels of signal strength, the body, and the brain's hints. A 14,068-weight net in plain Rust and NetBrain drive, aim and fire by them, five decisions a second, every choice masked to what can be done. CENSUS_NET seats a team of nets in the census, and without it every match is the same as before. A decision costs 7.8 µs on the Mac. A random team reads −19.30 ± 0.36 over 110 seeds, every map far below, as a net that knows nothing must. Alongside, dodge_horizon 2 passed both held-out reads on today's defaults (+1.82 ± 0.27 and +1.66 ± 0.29, 400 seeds each), with no map below, and make families holds: a candidate for Paul.

Index terms: sensor interface, multilayer perceptron, action masking, held-out replication, brain census.
