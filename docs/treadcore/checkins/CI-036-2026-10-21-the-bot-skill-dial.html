Abstract

Paul found the game hard. The only dial, --rookies N, was a yes or no a bot, and it reached no bot in a teamed match, which make run is; the player guide's advice did nothing. One weight, skill (1 in full, 0 a rookie), now blends each rookie trait: aim scatter and lead, drone delay, think rate, and under 0.5 no dodge, kite or storm. A Commander spoils its own orders at one place after its think. --skill and SKILL= deal it to the bots against the person; allies play in full. In the duel family the win rate falls steadily from 63% at 1 to 8% at 0.5, and every family's count is unchanged at 1. A match at 0.6 was left open on Paul's screen.

Index terms: difficulty, skill, rookies, playability.

I. Introduction

A. Intent

Paul's choice: "bot skill dial". A dial a person can set that makes the bots against them weaker by degrees.

B. The prompt

III. Methods

Code: weights.rs Skill; lib.rs RuleBot::skill, is_rookie, aim_skill, the blends; commander.rs set_settings, the dodge gate, blunt; client --skill (main.rs, session.rs); scripts/run.sh SKILL; Makefile help and export. Proof: make scenario S=scenarios/families/a-duel-in-the-open.toml RUNS=400 SET="skill=v" for v from 1 to 0; make families; make test. The window launched with --skill 0.6: no errors.

IV. Results

As section 67: duels outscored 63.0% at 1, 52.0% at 0.75, 24.5% at 0.6, 7.8% at 0.5, 0 under 0.5. Families unchanged at 1. Tests: brain 85 passed and 12 ignored; client 32; net 14; proto 3; sim 50.

V. Discussion

  1. A dial that did nothing. The rookie flag was built for plain RuleBots before teams were the default; nobody had checked it since. The guide sent a newcomer to it.
  2. A useful half. The dial is smooth from 1 to 0.5 and a wall below; a person's playtest decides whether 0.5 is easy enough.

For Paul: a match at skill 0.6 is open on your screen. Try it, and say whether 0.6 feels right; SKILL=0.5 make run is the easiest that still dodges. Commits are local until you say push.
