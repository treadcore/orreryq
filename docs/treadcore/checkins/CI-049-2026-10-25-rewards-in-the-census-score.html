Abstract

The census score now pays for pillbox fighting and gunnery. It counts a seat's direct hits on hostile tanks, its damage to hostile pillboxes, the neutral and hostile pillboxes its squad takes, its kills near a pillbox of its team, and a bonus when its team took every neutral pillbox. Each is a weight in ScoreWeights, and CENSUS_REWARDS=0 scores as before. A test checks each count; with the rewards off, an atlantic census reads as LR 83's to the digit. As first set they were 0.2 to 0.7 a seat against a score of about 17; Paul chose about a third of the score, so each was tripled. The personalities and the pillbox mechanics Paul described are logged as backlog 67 and 68.

Index terms: census, scoring, rewards, pillboxes.

IV. Results

In LR 84: the test; the old score to the digit; the rewards as first set 0.2 to 0.7 a seat on four atlantic seeds of 300 s, against a score of about 17; tripled at Paul's choice.

V. Discussion

What the rewards are worth decides what the census prefers. As first set, held ground led the score and the rewards were a nudge; tripled, pillbox fighting and gunnery are about a third of it. The census reads a difference between teams, so the term that matters is the one that varies, and a full census with the counts will show which of these do.
