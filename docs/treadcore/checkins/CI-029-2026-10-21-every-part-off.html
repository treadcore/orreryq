Abstract

Backlog 48, the starting numbers for the brain with every part on. LR 55 read every part on at -0.85 +/- 0.59 a seat against LR 54's defaults, and the hypothesis put that cost on one or two parts, by history the posture's lean and team_rotate. A new tool, make parts, turns each of 31 parts (17 capabilities, 14 weights) off from the all-on brain over the fair census at 12 seeds. One part reads past two standard errors, the dodge, and it pays (+5.20 +/- 1.91). The posture (+1.82) and team_rotate (+0.83) read as paying. The plan's halved weights (w_posture 0.25, swap_gap 0.6) read +0.61 +/- 0.60 at 48 seeds, and the table's lowest row halved (landfall_wait 5) -0.09 +/- 0.46. The hypothesis is not supported; the defaults stand. No brain code changed.

Index terms: tuning, ablation, census, every part on.

V. Discussion

  1. The cost is not a part. LR 55's -0.85 was 1.4 errors. Read with a table in which no part costs past two errors and two thirds read as paying, it is most likely the sum of small effects of both signs, or noise.
  2. The parts the history blamed pay. The posture's lean and team_rotate cost in earlier, partial brains; with the board, the front and the dodge beside them they read as gains.
  3. Twelve seeds rank; 48 decide. The table is a list of where to spend the night's censuses, which backlog 53 takes.
  4. A part that never acts. escort reads exactly 0 on naval.map; its proof needs a map where a builder crosses.
