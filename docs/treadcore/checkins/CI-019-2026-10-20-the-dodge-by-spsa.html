Abstract

With every team on the landfall defaults, half the deaths are in the dodge ashore (CI-016), and its numbers were first guesses. SPSA tuned dodge_horizon, dodge_margin and dodge_clear over 16 steps of 24 fresh seeds. It ended at 2.05 s, 1.52 and 3.73 tiles (defaults 4, 0.8, 2.5): start later, count more near misses, clear further. On 48 seeds the search never saw, that reads +1.24 ± 0.75 a seat, positive in every range. The trial team's dodges killed it 6.4% of the time, against 7.4% for the defaults. At 1.65 errors this is not past the two-error rule. A second fixed batch of 48 seeds decides, by a rule stated before it runs.

Index terms: SPSA, confirmation, optional stopping, reflexes.

V. Discussion

  1. A later dodge is a shorter detour. At 4 s a tank leaves its errand early and meets other fire. At 2 s it is caught by its own shell a little more often, but hit and killed less overall.
  2. The rule holds even for a good lead. All three ranges are positive and the meter agrees, but 1.65 errors is not two. The second batch is fixed in size and rule before it runs.
