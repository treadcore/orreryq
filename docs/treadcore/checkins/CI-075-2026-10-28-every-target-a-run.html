Abstract

This report covers steps 3 to 6 of the move from make to orrery.

  • Runs. Each of the 13 training and measurement targets is now a run of orrery's fleet queue, asked for by a button or by fleet-queue.py Q new KIND.
    • Censuses are played a machine each.
    • spsa and tune advance a step or a generation at a time, by their

    scripts' rules.

    • The learning harness runs as one job.
    • Recordings are delivered to the store, hashed.
  • The test. A tiny run of eight kinds worked end to end.
  • The Training view is in four pages (Fleet, Runs, Found, Net), with the newest change in one line and Found Improvement leaderboards, after Paul's word on the first version.
  • Sessions puts a resumable tmux session a machine in a grid, in 6×10.
  • Promotion is prepared on a branch, tested, its families read, and reaches main only on Paul's Approve.
  • The Makefile has no training.

The first promotion stopped at a test. Every strong candidate trades focus fire for shooting the near tank in range. That is Paul's decision.

V. Discussion

The fleet can now be driven from orrery alone:

  • the standing search;
  • every kind of run, by a button;
  • the families;
  • gathering, and shutting down;
  • promotion.

The first promotion found what the census cannot. The search's best weights change a behaviour the brain was built to have. The census, the families and the unit tests each catch a different thing, and here the unit test caught it. What happens to focus fire is Paul's choice.
