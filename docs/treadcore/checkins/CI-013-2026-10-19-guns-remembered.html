Abstract

CI-012 found that in 46% of the boats lost on the run for the beach, the team had seen the killer near there in the minute before, and lost it. This step gave the RuleBot a memory of hostile tanks out of sight, landfall_memory (default 0, Paul's choice), read by the landfall's pricing. With the memory at 30 s and the two earlier landfall weights on (landfall_shy 1, landfall_wait 10), the trial team gained 2.31 ± 0.58 a seat over 48 seeds, and its landfall deaths halved. This is the first landfall result outside the noise. Which of the three weights carries it is the next step.

Index terms: memory, partial observability, utility scoring, ablation.

V. Discussion

  1. The knowledge, not the rule. landfall_shy and landfall_wait each read null alone (CI-008, CI-010). With memory they are two of three weights in the best part measured since the dodge. A rule over what the brain sees can be right and still fail for want of what it should know.
  2. A death count, followed to its cause. CI-004 named the landfall, and four reads (CI-009, CI-011, CI-012) located the cause: guns seen and lost. The part that followed from them is the one that worked.
