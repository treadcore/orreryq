Abstract

The fleet's search had learned to score by building less: on 4 November every one of its 60 current candidates was held by the trade guard, the smallest trade +1.27 a seat for 1.48 fewer things built a team. The score now counts building at half a kill and counts the base: repairs, wood felled near it, its pillboxes' life and kills, and its guns ready. The score has a version, and the fleet reads anew under it. On the same 48 seeds the new score reads w8466 0.42 ± 0.15 a seat lower than the old one did.

I. Intent

orrery asked what should happen with the held line (promote one by hand, raise the floor to 2, keep holding, or count building in the score). Paul, word for word:

Count Building the score. give well defended pillboxes average time duration and longest best turrent survivial and its killrate and for bases, repair rate and usefull rate. in fact, workers mining forrests around bases should receive reward, because now base fills faster.

Then the numbers, each asked and chosen: a thing built 0.5, half a kill; all four pillbox measures; a base's useful rate as its guns ready; "Start anew" for the old reads; a repair 0.25; a load of wood 0.2 within 16 tiles; guns ready +3 a team; a base's reach 16 tiles of a depot.

III. Method

ledger.rs: seven counts on Outcome (serde defaults, so old ledger lines read), seven weights on ScoreWeights, the tally's turret kills, pillbox seconds a team and guns ready a second; the team's numbers written to each of its seats at records. fleet.rs: the base line. A test of the new counts (a repair each, a load near and one far and one empty, a turret kill, a minute of two guns one dry). 163 brain tests.

The rule, before the run: under the new score, w8466 against the defaults reads under its old-score read by more than two errors of the paired difference on the same seeds. Two censuses on this Mac, every map (the BSD paste shim), 48 seeds, 900 s, respawns 3, JOBS=8, w8466's own trial file; the old score by setting aside ledger.rs and fleet.rs for one build; paired seed by seed.

The new terms over the 432 teams of the new census, a team a match: built 21.5, repairs 4.7, wood near a base 1.45, pillbox life 0.13 and the longest 0.26 of the match, turret kills 0.05, guns ready 0.16.

V. Discussion

The score now sees what the trades gave away: w8466 loses 0.42 a seat by it, 2.8 errors. It does not see enough to make w8466 a loser, and it should not have to: a score is what is worth winning, and the guard is what a person will not trade. The floors stay; the fleet reads anew.

VI. What is not built

The new counts as a scenario's reward terms; a change to the guard's floors; any search over these weights, which are Paul's.

VII. Paul's decisions

Whether to commit and push this (CLAUDE.md); after the fleet's first reads under it, whether the guard's floors should move.
