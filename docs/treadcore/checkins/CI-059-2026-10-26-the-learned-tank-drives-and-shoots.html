Abstract

The learned tank was taught, by evolution strategies in Rust on eight cores, to drive to an enemy, a depot or a pillbox (999 of 999 held-out grounds) and to kill a still enemy with the direct gun while still going to the other two (999 of 999). Its senses grew from 101 to 133: instruments, a periscope of four exact contacts, and a channel for a friend's call; a team's shared queue of calls (help, attack, take) lets learned tanks ask one another for help, and speaks to scripted teammates as pings. With the periscope, phase 1 passed by generation 67 where the compass alone needed 500. Two of phase 3's rules were found empty by reading the untrained net first, and amended before training; a shoot-only net forgot how to go to a depot, so phase 3 trains all three grounds. In the census a team of the trained net reads -23.52 +/- 0.38, below a random net (-20.35 +/- 0.25): the rule fails, and says what to teach next. dodge_horizon 2 became the default after ten more held-out reads. Release 0.2.0 was prepared.

Index terms: evolution strategies, sensor interface, curriculum, catastrophic forgetting, multi-agent communication, release.

III. Methods

On the Mac (M1, 8 threads): make train (ES: 48 pairs, 12 grounds a generation, sigma 0.05, Adam 0.02, decay 0.0005); held out: 999 grounds from seed 9,000,000. Rules in build/train/NOTES.txt before each run, amendments dated in place. On the census machine at 7d0af54: build/net3/run.sh, two censuses (110 seeds from 500,000, every map, 24 jobs), then make converge with SINCE now. make families and make test on the Mac before the default commit.

V. Discussion

The grounds taught what they held: an exact bearing makes going to a point easy, and a curriculum keeps what it rehearses. They held nothing that shoots back, and the census measured the cost of that precisely: a net that charges is worse in a match than one that wanders. The next phase is the one the census asks for: live.
