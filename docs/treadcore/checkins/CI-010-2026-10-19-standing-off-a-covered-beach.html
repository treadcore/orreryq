Abstract

CI-009 found that boats on landfall die to guns they have seen. This step added a weight, landfall_wait (default 0, Paul's choice): a boat whose chosen beach a seen hostile tank covers waits out of that gun's reach before landing. At 10 s over 48 seeds it reads -0.51 ± 0.63 a seat. The landfall deaths did not fall, and the stand-off acted in under 0.07% of the trial team's time. The hypothesis is not confirmed: the guns that sink boats are rarely at the beach. Backlog 34 reads where they are.

III. Methods

A. The part

In RuleBot::landfall_order (lib.rs), after the beach best is chosen and when landfall_wait > 0: the nearest seen hostile tank within DIRECT_RANGE + 4 of best is the cover. If the crossing's stand-off (standoff, set on first use) is younger than the wait, the boat moves to gun + (me − gun).norm() × (DIRECT_RANGE + 6), clamped to the map, or holds if already that far. A move is not re-sent while the current goal is within 2 tiles of the target. standing marks the think; Commander::think_on reads it (standing_off) for stand_off. standoff resets ashore, at a respawn; standing also under a hop.

B. The test

a_boat_stands_off_a_covered_beach_for_its_wait: one beach 10 tiles off, a gun 8 tiles off beside it. At 0: a move to the beach. At 10: a move to 16 tiles from the gun, standing_off true; 11 s later, a move to the beach.

C. The census

landfall_wait = 10 as the trial weights, 48 seeds in three ranges, naval.map, 900 s, respawns 3, the fair census, CENSUS_ACTS.

V. Discussion

  1. Read how often a part acts before its score. A part that fires 0.07% of the time cannot move a score, and its null result says nothing about the idea. The status codes made the activation rate a one-line read.
  2. Three parts, one wrong premise. landfall_shy, the remembered guns, and the stand-off each assumed where the gun stands. Backlog 34 measures that before a fourth part.
