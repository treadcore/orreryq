Abstract

A candidate promotes only if the rules with its dials stand on every gauntlet floor, and every floor is today's success, so a generation can only go forward.

I. Intent

Paul, word for word: "adjust the floors to be what we have now as sucess, so we can create generations from here forwards", and, asked whether the floors should guard every promotion: "Yes, a guard on promotions".

II. Decision log

III. Method

learn.rs: GAUNTLET_BRAINS=scripted, with_dials. The rules' shares of section 142 (200 seeds) against every floor; the promotion job's check run on that read (holds) and on the read with w_shooter = 0 (G20 51%, under 0.60: held).

IV. Results

Today's rules hold all twenty-two floors. A candidate that undid section 142 would be held at G20.

V. Discussion

The floors were the nets' targets (section 102) and the boundary's (section 141); they are now the line's ratchet, each raised as the rules earn it.

VI. What is not built

The nets' and styles' own floors; the floors raised by themselves.
