Abstract

From 1 to 4 November 13 of 36 promotions failed, every one on a unit test that read the brain's defaults, none on a family. The unit tests now play a pinned brain, the defaults of 4 November written down once in pinned.weights. The families and the gauntlet still play the defaults they judge. A lint holds every unit test to the rule. With the change, the failed promotions' moves and a move of every searchable weight pass every brain test.

I. Intent

Paul: "commit push. then 1 and 2." Item 1 was the unit tests that lock today's defaults, found when Paul asked to "look into making blockages minized".

III. Method

Two worktrees, main at 43f9f67 and main with the change. Each move is written by scripts/set-defaults.py as a promotion writes it, then cargo test -p treadcore-brain --release --lib runs. The moves: those of all 13 failed promotions (ten distinct) and a stress move. The stress move shifts every searchable weight by a fifth of its range, from its default toward the farther end (109 weights).

V. Discussion

A promotion can no longer fail on a test of how a part works. The families and the gauntlet carry the whole judgement of the defaults, as they were meant to. The cost is the thirteen scenario files at the top of scenarios/. Under the stress move two of them failed (the dodge). They now play the pinned brain, so a promotion that broke the dodge would pass them; only a family or a gauntlet file would stop it. None of the 13 failures of 1 to 4 November was one of these files.

VI. What is not built

A reading of the top-level scenario files at the candidate's defaults, as a gate with a floor.

VII. Paul's decisions

Whether the top-level scenario files should also be read at the candidate's defaults, as the gauntlet's files are.
