Abstract

The night's best candidate, grove/w10040, moved ping_style from 0 to 2 and failed one brain test. That test's helper read its weights text over the defaults. LR 147 had pinned the brain the unit tests play, but its lint let a parse over the defaults through. Its stress moved the numbers and never a choice or a switch. A weights text now reads over the pinned brain, the fleet's role tests ask under it, and a scenario's switches read over its own brain. set-defaults.py now writes the tactics' true/false fields and what a switch makes of them. A promotion of any of five weights or switches would have failed on those fields. Stressed over every promotable choice, every switch and every true/false weight, the line failed six tests before. After the change, everything passed except two assertions that the kite and castle switches are on by default. Paul had them dropped.

I. Intent

Paul: "fix the test." The test was commander::tests::with_pings_on_as_built_a_bot_comes_to_any_ping, on which r0185, the promotion of w10040, failed at 05:44 on bosporus.

III. Method

Two states of a worktree of the line the fleet runs, f04f157 (main aebeb1d with r0179's bio 0 to 3): as it is, and with the change. Each move is written by that worktree's scripts/set-defaults.py as a promotion writes it, then cargo test -p treadcore-brain --release --lib runs. The moves:

  • w10040's ping_style = 2;
  • the 13 choices the fleet promotes, each moved one, two, three and four versions on from its default (modulo its count);
  • every one of the 18 switches flipped;
  • kite, shell_boxes and longshot_any set to 0;
  • LR 147's moves: its stress and the ten distinct moves of the 13 failed promotions.

The scripts and files are in the session's scratchpad (lr148/replay.sh, *.weights).

V. Discussion

The pinned brain held for everything that built a brain by name and let by everything that built one from a text or a thread's setting. A promotion is the only thing that moves the defaults, so every way a test reads them shows only when the fleet moves the right number. The stresses are the check that does not wait for the fleet: they now move every kind of thing a promotion moves. One failure was not a test reading the defaults. It was a writer that did not write what it promised, and its test was right to stop it.

VI. What is not built

The lint reads a test's body whole. A test that sets with_settings for one call and not another passes it; only the stress finds that.

VII. Paul's decisions

The kite and castle switches: default_weights_are_todays_constants asserted both on by default, with no decree in RANGES to hold them. Paul: "drop assertions." Both went, and so did the assertion on the same line that the bot does not smooth. The round-trip test that a smooth promoted to 1 would have failed now picks values off the defaults. Every searchable weight at its min and at its max then found the slider test's zero step and five tests' wingmen on the defaults; both are mended.

Whether to commit and push this. On main it lets w10040 be tried again, since orrery retries a candidate whose tests failed once main moves.
