Abstract

A review, no run. It does three things. It lists the methods the census gained in LR 78 to 86: the sequential census, families as a guard, parts taken apart, the squad and spending meters, rungs.py and traits.py, rewards in the score, and worktrees on the machine. It sets the gains beside how they were found. Continuous tuning by SPSA read flat (LR 74: -0.00 +/- 0.14). Turtle, a hand-set style, read +1.40. The cowboy, new behaviour, read +11.62. And it plans the census as a learning loop: the paired score as the objective, fresh seeds as batches, 400 held-out seeds as the test set, families as constraints, the NOTES rule as preregistration. The steps, in order: make dead weights act and screen every weight's slope; evaluate against a league of fixed opponents; run MAP-Elites over the personalities with traits.py's signature as the descriptor; batch search on the 24 threads; weights by map features.

Index terms: methods, brain census, machine learning, SPSA, quality-diversity, MAP-Elites, league training.

V. Discussion

The census is already most of a learning system: it has an objective, fresh data each step, a held-out test, constraints and preregistration. What it lacks is a search that proposes behaviour rather than numbers, and opponents that stay put. The personalities Paul asked for are the bridge. A quality-diversity search keeps one of each, tuned, and plain to see by construction.
