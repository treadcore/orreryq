Abstract

The making of treadcore is now documented as part of its MIT source. PROCESS.md describes the pair, how a prompt becomes a commit, where every conversation is kept, how the brain is designed with weights, scenario families, the census and held-out reads, the fifteen days, and what the process caught. The three pages that had existed only as shared links are reports of the site: the weight ledger (rebuilt as a static page from its data, with the day's two default changes), the big-lizard map and the learned tank. Guided training is planned: one scenario file a behaviour, read by the families for the scripted brain and by the trainer for the net on the same seeds, and a series of ten training maps that place situations predictably. The run stays on the census machine all day.

Index terms: open-source process, reproducibility, scenario families, curriculum, documentation.
