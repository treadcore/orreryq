Abstract

Every strong candidate of the search traded focus fire for shooting the near tank in range (LR 116 D).

  • The scalar. By Paul's rule that choice is now a scalar, focus_blend. At 0 the tank shoots the near tank in range; at 1 the wounded one a teammate is on. 0.5, the default, plays as before, bit for bit, behind a Caps switch.
  • The test holds the two ends.
  • A fault, found and closed. Checking that the default plays as before found that a shared cargo target folder can hand a census another commit's binary. The census now plays the binary its machine built for that commit.

I. Introduction

A. Intent

Turn an either/or the census found into a weight the census can set, without changing today's play.

B. The prompt

Paul's answer to the question "What should the brain do?":

Make it a scalar

III. Methods

  1. weights.rs: FocusBlend in weights!; FOCUS_BLEND 21 in caps!; from_weights (the blend at 0.5 when off).
  2. tactics.rs: the focus_blend field, Tactics::default(), rate(), and the test.
  3. The determinism check: brain_census, the defaults, 2 seeds of 120 s on naval.map, CENSUS_LEDGER=0. The old binary was 0e257c8's census-bin on the Mac; the new, a fresh build of the tree. The comparison is the team lines.
  4. The bisection: a scratch worktree at 49f2f3b with each part added in turn (the table line, the field and mapping, the Caps line, the scoring), each built and compared.
  5. census.sh: CENSUS_BIN. orrery tools/plans/treadcore.py: CENSUS_BIN=$C/census-bin on every census; a promotion's CARGO_TARGET_DIR=$W/target; same_defaults by the table's numbers (defaults_at).

V. Discussion

focus_blend costs nothing at its default and gives the census a dial. The search will read its half and double without being asked.

The fault matters more than the feature. A census is only as true as the binary it names, and a shared build folder could have mislabelled one silently. Nothing measured so far was touched: every commit the fleet built had the same brain source. From this commit on, the brain changes, and the census now plays exactly the binary its commit made.
