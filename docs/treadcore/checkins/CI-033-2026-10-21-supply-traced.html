III. Methods

fleet::tests::supply_trace (ignored): for each of SUPPLY_SEEDS (6) seeds a thread with a 256 MB stack; the census's world (Scenario with the fleet's teams, naval.map, SUPPLY_RESPAWNS 3, no time limit) run to SUPPLY_SECS (1,800) or the end; every seat fleet::brain. Every 10 s each side earns 1 a live pillbox and 2 a depot; every 20 s 1 a powered structure; 10 for a TankDestroyed of another team's tank by its own seat, 5 for a PillboxCaptured from another team. Printed: by side, supply by source, the seconds at 60 and 120, every 5 minutes; in all, supply a team-minute, the shares, the median second at 60, the match length. Log: build/supply/trace1.log.

IV. Results

As PROPOSAL-UPGRADES.md section 7: 48.8 supply a team-minute (7.3 to 92.5 by team); 60 by 18 of 18 teams, median 97 s; depots 60%, pillboxes 21%, kills 19%, captures under 1%, structures 0%; matches 18.0 min on average (16 to 23). Tests: brain 84 passed and 12 ignored; client 32; net 14; proto 3; sim 50.
