Abstract

A profile of the census found one function, World::visible_to, taking 58% of its time. Four changes make the census 2.6 times faster with every match the same: the old and new binaries were compared match for match on the Mac and on the census machine, and with make families. They are deployed, mid-day, to the census machine. The day's queue and a planned night were folded into one run, make converge. Each generation it reads every census into a ledger, fits each weight's curve, and plays the trials the fit suggests. A trial past two errors is read twice more on fresh seeds. A faster forecast for the dodge (dodge_own_route) saved 1.2 times more but read −6.48 ± 1.56 on big-lizard, so it ships off. CUDA would not speed the census; the GPU is for training a network later. Paul set that aside.

Index terms: profiling, determinism, response surface, successive halving, brain census.

III. Methods

The profile (Mac): a census run in the background, sampled with sample PID 20, its call tree attributed by caller. A build with the linker's symbol merging off did not name the merged symbol. Its caller in the tree (route_kept) did.

The identity check, after each change:

CENSUS_MAP=<eleven maps>,gen CENSUS_RESPAWNS=3 CENSUS_SEED0=170000 \ CENSUS_SEEDS=6 CENSUS_SECS=300 cargo test -q -p treadcore-brain \ --release -- --ignored brain_census --nocapture

stderr compared with diff, the "us a seat-tick" figures and the harness's "running for over 60 seconds" lines taken out. On the census machine: the census binaries of 20261026-102652 (before) and 20261026-104153 (after), CENSUS_WEIGHTS column_past=1, 4 seeds of 300 s.

The run on the census machine: nohup setsid make converge; resumed after the route census with RESUME=build/converge/STAMP. The route census: TRIAL=build/route/own.weights SEEDS=110 SEED0=170000 MAP=all JOBS=24 make census, its rule in build/route/NOTES.txt first.

V. Discussion

Every change was measured against a census that matches to the character, and that check is what made a 2.6 times speed-up safe on a machine running a day of results. The two tries that bought nothing show the profile alone was not enough; each change had to be timed. The route change is the reverse case: a real saving that alters play, and the census found what a forecast that looks more faithful costs on one map. The run now does on its own, generation after generation, what the last three days did by hand.
