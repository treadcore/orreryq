V. Discussion

A screen at half and double reads a weight only where the weight has something to decide. Both inert weights sit behind a comparison with another number (w_flank; a mend's hurt), so their range of action is set by that other number, not by their own default. The day's arms test exactly that. The screen of the remaining 68 will show how many more are like them; dead weights then leave every search.

The study's main finding is about cost: a network for this game is small and the sim is not, so a GPU would mostly wait for CPUs. A learned brain is feasible with what treadcore has. Its first step, the bridge, is a day of code. Behaviour cloning from the scripted brain answers Paul's aiming question better than shaped rewards alone.
