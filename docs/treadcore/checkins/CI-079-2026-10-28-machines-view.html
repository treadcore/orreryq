Abstract

orrery takes a fresh Ubuntu install to a fleet node from its window:

  • probe it;
  • apply its setup recipes, each behind a screen of details and a typed name, with root typed by Paul in the machine's Sessions tile;
  • join it.

whitewolf (GTX 1080) was set up by the new one-run script and joined. This Mac is in the view by its hostname.

I. Introduction

A. Intent

The steps a new machine went through by hand become orrery's process, safe to repeat and hard to misapply.

III. Methods

  1. orrery tools/fleet-node.sh:
    • build tools;
    • rustup for SUDO_USER;
    • treadcore over HTTPS;
    • cuda-node.sh or tmux and copal-tm;
    • fleet-login.sh --role;
    • fleet-poweroff-setup.sh;
    • the coordinator's two lines.
  2. orrery tools/cuda-node.sh: modprobe, else a reboot note, before the check.
  3. orrery tools/machines.py: the registry, PROBE and LOCAL_PROBE, RECIPES, applies and applied, stage with sums read back, and join.
  4. orrery src/machines.rs: the view and its confirmation; sessions.rs run() (Ask::Window); nav.rs View::Machines; main.rs: the status thread, asks on threads, the hand-off, --frame-view machines.
  5. Checks:
    • 340 tests;
    • review frames of the page and the confirmation;
    • every machine probed;
    • whitewolf's host key over its address compared with its key by

V. Discussion
