Abstract

The parts:

  • The engine, orrery's tools/fleet-queue.py: a queue, a lane per machine, and a keeper that keeps them running and stops adding after five failures in a row.
  • The planner, treadcore's scripts/fleet-plan.py, which fills the queue with trials of the scripted brain's 110 weights and rounds of the learned net, in turn.
  • The controls: stop adding, and collect results and stop now. They are commands, and buttons in orrery's Training view.

The sample run, on calber, columbia, nasser and the Mac:

  • It built the queue's commit everywhere.
  • It ran a net round: trained on calber's GPU, judged on columbia, candidate +0.64 ± 0.40 over 4 seeds, so the best stays.
  • It ran 70 weight trials in five minutes. One read past two errors and was queued for its held-out reads.
  • It found two faults on the way.

V. Discussion

The fleet can now be left to train treadcore. It keeps itself fed, keeps itself running, and stops when told, with its results kept.

The sample's numbers say only that it works. Four seeds of 2 minutes cannot tell a weight's effect from luck. The real settings (55 seeds of 900 s, and 400 held out) are what can.
