Abstract

crucible's GPU islands have trained under the game's rules since the evening of 3 November, with 55 inputs and two hidden layers of 48. The game's bridge to crucible (style 7, the Duelist, LR 126) read only the old arena's 35-input hybrid. The bridge now reads a world champion with its twenty world inputs built from what a tank in the game sees, as crucible builds them, and drives as crucible's world tank: a heading error, no reverse. It ships as a new style, 17, the Gladiator (world milestone M0015), beside the hybrid Duelist. On the same 48 seeds the Gladiator reads +4.99 ± 2.08 a seat over the Duelist on every map (+11.56 ± 1.75 on the generated maps). The fleet reads a champion not yet shipped by GLADIATOR=file, and orrery's bridge reads each new world milestone against the shipped one; shipping it is a commit, pressed.

III. Method

Code in source/treadcore-brain/src/styles.rs: Champion { nin, h, w } and Champion::world; world_senses, scripted_world, shell_at; the world branch of Duelist::duel; slot 17 in ROSTER; gladiator_file. weights.rs: bot_style's choices end in gladiator. Seven tests new (the sizes, the arithmetic at 55-48-48-4, the world yardstick, eyes past TANK_VISION, a pillbox that has the tank and one of the side's own, an open gate with no throttle holding the tank). The brain crate's 178 tests pass.

The rule, before either census: the Gladiator is better than the Duelist if, on the same 48 seeds, its read against the defaults is higher by more than two standard errors of the paired difference.

Censuses: scripts/census.sh on this Mac, JOBS=8, SEEDS=48, SEED0=0, SECS=900, RESPAWNS=3, TRIAL a file of one line (bot_style = 17, then bot_style = 7), the defaults as the base. The pair is read seed by seed from each job's a seed: list (the jobs split the seeds alike). The first pair's MAP=all played the generated map only: BSD paste needs a -, which orrery's census jobs supply and a census by hand on a Mac does not. The second pair ran with that shim and played every map.

V. Discussion

A champion of the toy arena, read in the game, cost its team 7.5 a seat on every map (13.8 on the generated maps); a champion of the world arena, read through the same gate, costs it 2.5 (2.3). The world islands trained against pillboxes and inside the game's own reach and speed, and the bridge now tells it the same things: that is the whole of the change, and the read says it was most of the gap. What remains is the Gladiator's own gap to the rules (it still reads under the defaults), which is the islands' to close and the bridge's to measure each time a milestone is taken.

VI. What is not built

crucible's own sense run on a game state, for parity to the decimal (it runs only on a card); the bridge's first reads on the fleet (they need this commit on main); the GPU behaviour-cloning stage (Paul's step 3), its net and its judge not yet chosen.
