Abstract

A census of fixed length spends as many seeds on a clear call as on a close one. LR 72 found the dodge settled by seed 20 of 176. This check-in replays every finished census on the census machine (44 of them, 11 304 seeds, LR 72 to 76) under two group-sequential tests with eight looks, each bound set by simulation to the fixed census's false alarm rate (P(|Z| > 2) = 0.0455). O'Brien-Fleming reached 43 of the 44 verdicts on 76% of the seeds; Pocock spent 69% and lost five. The one O'Brien-Fleming miss, LR 73's -column, stopped outside at 100 seeds (z -3.08) and read -1.02 at 200: a false early stop, at about the rate the design allows. By the rule set before the replay the test is not adopted. An early stop as a mark for a re-read is left to Paul.

Index terms: brain census, sequential testing, O'Brien-Fleming, Pocock, group-sequential design, false alarm rate.

III. Methods

The data. On the census machine, every brain_census: difference line (each job's seed differences) under build/census, switches, board, front, front2 and sitepoor, copied to the Mac. Left out: build/spsa (each step a census of a few seeds) and the round robin under way (build/census/20261024-211723).

The test. scripts/sequential.py DIR.... A census's seeds, its jobs interleaved by round; looks at n_k = round(k N / 8), k = 1 to 8; z_k the mean of the first n_k over its standard error. It stops at the first k with |z_k| > c s(k): O'Brien-Fleming s(k) = sqrt(8 / k), Pocock s(k) = 1. c is the 0.9545 quantile of max_k |S_k / sqrt(k)| / s(k) over 200 000 Gaussian random walks (seed 59): 2.117 and 2.557. The verdict is "out" at a stop, "in" at the end without one; it is compared with the fixed census's |z_N| > 2 and its sign.

To repeat. On the machine:

V. Discussion

The saving is real and comes from the large effects, as LR 72 expected. The miss is the price of looking: at a 4.55% false alarm rate over 22 censuses inside two errors, one false stop is the expected count. The fixed census pays the same rate, but at the end. What a sequential census gives up is the 100 more seeds in which a run of early luck regresses. The rule set first says not to adopt it. One form keeps both: stop early, but treat an early "out" as the reason for a re-read on fresh seeds before a default changes, as LR 76 already did for team_rotate. That changes how a verdict is reached, which is Paul's call.
