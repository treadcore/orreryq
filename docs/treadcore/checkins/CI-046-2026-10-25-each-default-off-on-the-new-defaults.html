Abstract

LR 77 changed the brain's defaults to turtle with team_rotate off. LR 76's census of each part switched off was read against the old defaults, so it was run again at the new ones: twelve rows, 200 fresh seeds, every map. No row is past two errors toward a change, so by the rule set first no default changes. The dodge (-4.76 +/- 0.28 off), the posture (-0.51 +/- 0.22) and priced_crossing (-0.47 +/- 0.15) pay. team_rotate on reads -0.24 +/- 0.23, costing atlantic -4.13. The rest sit inside the noise. While it ran, the twelve single scenarios were read over 200 seeds each on the Mac: ten held on every seed, and the two cut from the ready-gun family held 80%, that family's own rate.

III. Methods

On the machine: SWITCHES="-forget -board team_rotate -priced_crossing -column -cycle -island_site -escort -bridge_jobs -duck -posture -dodge" SEEDS=200 SEED0=100000 SPLIT=2 JOBS=24 MAP=all make switches, 900 s, 3 respawns, a frozen binary; each row's map split by scripts/pool.sh DIR/ROW. IV. Results

In LR 81: the table and the map splits. Scenarios over 200 seeds: ten at 200 of 200; a-dodging-tank-holds-its-fire and a-tank-ducks-out-of-reach-in-time at 160 of 200 (80.0% +/- 2.8%), against the ready-gun family's 83.4% and its 80% floor.

V. Discussion

The new defaults rest on the same parts as the old: the dodge first by far, then the posture and the priced crossing. The posture and the board pay a little less on a turtle than on the old brain, though not past two errors of the difference. Turtle's weights already hold the base, which is the posture's own lean. Atlantic is the map where the board, the posture and team_rotate are each worth the most, the same as before.
