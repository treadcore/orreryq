Abstract

What was done:

  • The fleet's spare CPUs (calber, the Mac, columbia; nasser joining) recorded the scripted brain in 1,200 matches in 14 minutes. Every shard was streamed to calber and checked by sha256 at both ends.
  • calber's 1080 Ti trained bc2 on all 47 million training samples in 226 s, streaming them through the card.
  • A batch sweep found the card fastest at batch 32,768; larger batches are slower.

What was found:

  • Twenty times the data moved the drive head's held-out agreement from 65.5% to 66.6%. Rule part 2 passed.
  • In the census against bc1, paired on one binary: bc2 is 3.42 +/- 0.41 a seat better than bc1, paired on one binary (rule part 3 passed), and level with the official net map7 (+0.57 +/- 0.37, inside the noise).
  • The gauntlet and the match reading show what cloning learns: to survive, not to go anywhere. The clones die once in 40 matches and pass none of the gauntlet's journeys.
  • So the senses, not the amount of data, set the clone's ceiling.

Errors and false starts:

  • an equivalence check bound written too tight in LR 107, widened before the run;
  • pkill -f "nvidia-smi dmon" killed its own ssh session (bc2 was already written);
  • the gauntlet run first needed the extra nets;
  • the census's binary differed from bc1's because the fleet's worktrees shared columbia's target/ (bc1 read again, as the rule said).

V. Discussion

The fleet works and its time is accounted for. The card, made efficient, reads all the data in four minutes.

The finding is about cloning. More examples of a choice the net cannot see the reason for teach it the safe guess: stand still, hold fire, survive. The drive label is the sector of a route the scripted brain planned over the whole map. Two ways forward, Paul to choose:

  1. Give the net the plan as senses: its route's next waypoint and its goal, as a sector and a distance.
  2. Train on the census's own score (PPO from bc2), which needs no labels at all.

The official net stays nets/map7.bin until a candidate beats it in the census (LAB-REPORT-FLEET VII).
