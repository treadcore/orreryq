Abstract

Backlog 23, the honest tuner. The census now takes a weights file for its baseline teams. A new script, make spsa, tunes named weights by simultaneous perturbation: one paired census a step, plus side against minus side, on fresh seeds. Its planted test detuned landfall_memory to 5 s and asked SPSA to bring it back toward 30. After eight steps it stood at 3.69 s, with every step's reading inside its error. The test most likely sat on a flat: memory acts on sightings 10 to 60 s old, and the perturbations reached only 3 to 7.5 s. It runs again with wider perturbations.

Index terms: stochastic approximation, SPSA, planted test, noisy optimisation.

III. Methods

scripts/spsa.sh: for k = 1..K, a_k = A/(k + 1)^0.602, c_k = C/k^0.101; δ_i = ±1 at random; the plus file θ_i e^(c_k δ_i) is CENSUS_WEIGHTS and the minus file θ_i e^(−c_k δ_i) is CENSUS_BASE_WEIGHTS. The census runs in JOBS ranges side by side and its per-seed differences are pooled to d̄ and its error. log θ_i moves by min(MAXSTEP, max(−MAXSTEP, a_k d̄ / (2 c_k δ_i))). Planted: SET="landfall_memory=5" TUNE=landfall_memory STEPS=8 SEEDS=12, naval.map, 900 s, respawns 3, seeds 1,000 to 1,095.

V. Discussion

A planted test has to put the weight where the slope is, not only away from the optimum. The census resolves about 1.2 a seat at 12 seeds, and the gradient between 3 and 7.5 s of memory is far below that.
