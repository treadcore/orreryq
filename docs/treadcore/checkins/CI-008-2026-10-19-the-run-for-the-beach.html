Abstract

CI-004 found 20% of the trial team's deaths on landfall, the boat's run for a beach, most of them sunk by direct fire. This step added a weight, landfall_shy (default 0, Paul's choice), that prices the tiles of the run inside a seen hostile gun's reach. At 1 over 48 seeds it reads +0.65 ± 0.69 a seat, and the landfall deaths did not change (169 against 165 on the same 16 seeds). The hypothesis is not confirmed. Sight and direct range are both 10 tiles, so a boat sees the gun that sinks it only once it is in its reach. Backlog 32 prices the guns the team has seen instead.

Index terms: partial observability, utility scoring, boats, weights.

III. Methods

A. The part

RuleBot::landfall_order (lib.rs) scores each reachable shore tile b as dist(me, b) + danger(b) + toward(b) + 4 × shy × exposed(b). exposed(b) cuts the line from the boat to b into ⌈dist⌉ steps and counts, in tiles, the steps within DIRECT_RANGE + 4 of a seen hostile tank or PILLBOX_RANGE + 2 of a hostile pillbox. It returns 0 at once when shy is 0, so default play is unchanged by construction. Commander::set_settings copies W::LandfallShy into the inner RuleBot.

B. The test

a_shy_boat_runs_for_the_beach_clear_of_the_guns: a beach 30 tiles away whose run passes a gun 12 tiles off the line, and one 40 away with a gun 15 beyond it. Scores at 0: 66 and 82, the near beach chosen. At 1, 15 exposed tiles add 60: 126 and 82, the far beach chosen.

C. The census

The defaults dumped with landfall_shy = 1, 48 seeds in three ranges, naval.map, 900 s, respawns 3, the fair census, CENSUS_ACTS on.

V. Discussion

  1. What the code already does should come before the hypothesis. The old score already steered boats off every gun they could see. The step's own first reading showed it, and the census confirmed it.
  2. The limit is the information, not the decision. A gun is seen at its own range. Better choices need better knowledge: what a teammate saw, kept for a while. That is the team board's job (CI-001 A1), and the first use for it that a death count supports.
