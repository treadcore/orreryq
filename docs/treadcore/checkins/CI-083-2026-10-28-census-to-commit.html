Abstract

The path from a discovered coefficient to the brain in a fresh clone is drawn with real commits and times. It is completed:

  • Micro versions: each promotion is tagged brain-RID.
  • Do it now: a button that syncs, promotes and pushes.
  • Re-reads: older candidates are read again on today's defaults, so the loop does not stop at "0 current".

crucible became grove's second job: a GPU lane per card, the three cards as islands, from a button on Projects.

On Results, the candidates are filtered and scrolled, and the Net page draws every sense and score as a bar, with an explanation on hover.
