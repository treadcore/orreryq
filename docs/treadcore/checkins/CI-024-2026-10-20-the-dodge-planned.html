Abstract

The dodge, the reflex behind 43% of deaths (LR 27), still let a tenth of ashore dodges be caught by their own shell after LR 52's water fix. Scenario families, single files whose geometry is drawn from the run's seed, measured today's dodge at 89.6% (a tank rolling at full speed) and 96.2% (at rest) over 1,000 seeds each, in three seconds. Traces named two failures: a dodge point behind a rolling tank, reached by a looping two-leg route, and a tank that arrived early and was sent back under its shell by its errand. A motion model, drive_to, foresees the world's route and the sim's rules of motion exactly. Two weights built on it, dodge_plan and dodge_release, take both families to 100%. In the fair census they cut own-shell catches ashore by 40% in every run, and read +0.92 +/- 0.47 a seat on naval.map, +1.20 +/- 0.60 on a generated map, and +0.70 +/- 0.66 on 48 fresh seeds: not confirmed alone, +0.81 +/- 0.40 pooled. Both stay off, for Paul's choice.

Index terms: reflexes, motion models, foresight, scenario testing, confirmation on fresh seeds.

III. Methods

A. The brain's parts explored

  • Dodge (lib.rs): fields dodged, until, watch, tune (now six numbers: horizon, margin, clear, hold, plan, release), began, and the new landing (where and when the shell dodged last lands).
  • Dodge::order_on: the trigger (an indirect hostile shell landing within the splash and dodge_margin of the tank or of where it will be at its speed, between 2 ticks and dodge_horizon out); the four points of LR 52, each SPLASH_RADIUS + dodge_clear from the impact; the reachability test fits. With dodge_plan at 0.5 or more it hands them to planned.
  • Dodge::planned (new): candidates in order (today's four; straight on, where the heading leaves the circle of reach round the impact, if it meets it; twelve round the impact every 30 degrees), each that fits driven by drive_to for the shell's flight; the first to end at least SPLASH_RADIUS + dodge_margin from the impact is taken; if none does, the one ending farthest off.
  • drive_to (new, free function): the route by Map::route_by(pos, to, boat, no danger); per tick, as drive_tank in the sim: the ground's cost (BOAT_MOVE_COST afloat on water), the heading error, easing past TURN_BRAKE_ANGLE to TURN_CRAWL, the pivot past TURN_PIVOT, braking before the next waypoint's corner, a boat's tighter turn, momentum at TANK_ACCEL and TANK_BRAKE, the hull turned at most TANK_TURN a tick, and a stop where the next step is not open. It leaves out the world's price on pillboxes' reach and known mines, and a re-plan after a block.
  • Dodge::release (new): with dodge_release at 0.5 or more and the dodged shell still in flight, every Command::Move in the think's output whose drive_to end at the landing tick is inside the splash and the margin is struck.
  • Commander::think_released (new): think_map, then release unless this tick's act is the dodge itself; when one is struck, last_move is cleared so the ladder asks again. Both think and think_on go through it.
  • Commander::think_map's dodge branch (read, unchanged): the dodge and holding before every rung; holding ends on arrival at dodge_hold 0, the opening F2 used.
  • Commander::set_settings: passes the six numbers to Dodge::tune.
  • The sim, read: World::step's Command::Move (the world routes), Map::route_by (eight neighbours; a waypoint at each change of direction, at tile centres; the last replaced by the target), drive_tank, and the shell's impact drawn at launch.
  • meter.rs: the dodge counts by group, trial and the rest, disjoint.

B. The scenario form (scenario.rs)

[[tank]]: speed, heading in degrees (0 east, 90 south) or "any", drive (the rolling goal's distance, 20). [[shell]]: offset, scatter, from_any, lands_in = [lo, hi]. The dice: Rng::new(seed ^ 0x5ce4a210), the run's seed (0 for a file run alone). seed = n at the top pins a file to one of many's seeds. The trace: each tank's route end and legs, each indirect shell's landing and time.

The families (scenarios/families/a-rolling-tank-under-a-shell.toml, a-standing-tank-under-a-shell.toml): two seats, 4.5 s; seat 0 a Commander on Auto at full speed on any heading, or at rest; a shell of seat 1 from 30 tiles in any direction, landing within 2.5 tiles of seat 0's tank in 1.5 to 3.5 s. A run holds if the tank is never hit. make scenario S=file RUNS=1000 SET="...", seeds 1 to 1,000.

V. Discussion

  1. The impact is known, so the line is not. Every dodge since step 10 chose its side by the shell's line; once fired, the only facts that matter are where the shell lands, when, and how this tank moves.
  2. Foresee the world, not a picture of it. A plan on a straight line changed nothing, because the world routes. The model that worked copies the sim's rules, and its test proves it exact.
  3. A hold should know why it holds. dodge_hold held every errand and cost 1.18 a seat; dodge_release holds only the ones foreseen under the shell.
  4. The mechanism replicates where the score does not yet. Own-shell catches fell by 40% on every set of seeds. The score moved +0.7 to +1.2 each time, below what 48 seeds resolve alone. About 95 fresh seeds would settle it.
  5. Checks that cannot fail. A goal that runs no brain, an assertion that tested the wrong thing, a pin one runner skipped, a meter's groups misread: each read as a pass or a number until run broken or questioned.
