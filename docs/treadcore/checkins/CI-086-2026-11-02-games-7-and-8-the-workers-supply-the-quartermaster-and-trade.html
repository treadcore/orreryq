II. What was built

Game 7. Wells of oil as the deposits, pumped by the squad, unloaded at the side's refinery; a refinery with oil halves the burn and is a fuel stop, one without is neither; on a map with wells a depot's fuel comes from a stocked refinery within thirty tiles, an oil a stop, else a trickle. The workers: a smelter's shells and a depot's shells for its pillboxes on a figure that walks the map's route, five at a time, one leaving every ten seconds; a splash loses it, a hostile tank that drives up takes it. Supply as PROPOSAL-UPGRADES 1.1 had it, spent as one side; the depot's stores (F6, sixty): thirty of each kind, made twice a second. The guard as quartermaster (city.rs, city_plan): a harvester by the wood; the stores; the road to the smelter; the industrial zone taken, the plant first; a refinery where there is oil; a data centre and a workshop. The oil run beside the ore run. The top strip shows the side's supply, shells, ore, oil and workers. The fleet's three maps carry their own deposits and wells.

Game 8. Convoys of supply between a side's depots, one a depot a minute to its nearest partner, by road or over the water between ports; both ends paid by the distance; a convoy is a worker a raider takes. The scripted brain covers a convoy of its own from a hostile near its road and raids one of another side it sees.

The gauntlets. G13 ore to the smelter, G14 oil to the refinery, G15 the convoy comes through, G16 the raider takes the convoy, G17 the chain to the city (deposit, factory, port, city). The scenario form places deposits, wells and structures, knows road, and expects and rewards ore, smelted, oil, oil_in, delivered, convoys, taken and lost. Seventeen new senses for the learned tank; old nets and the shipped transform widened.

III. What was read

  • Gauntlet, ten seeds: the scripted brain 10 of 10 on all five; the nets 0 on the three that need the squad, 7 to 10 on the two that a gun or a wander passes. The table is LR 130 and the lab report's section VI.
  • End to end, nine bots on the naval map for ten minutes, under game 8: 3 smelters, 36 ore, 32 smelted, 63 workers left and 62 arrived, 2 stores bought, 7 city works, no tank at a dry depot past 5 s.
  • Censuses of two seeds: supply 258 to 583 a team under game 7; under the first cut of trade 16,951 for one team, which is why trade is one convoy a depot a round at half the rate.
  • Simulator 66 tests, brain 154, the workspace and its tests compile.

IV. What is not built
