Abstract

orrery's window now wears treadcore's look:

  • brushed steel plates, from the same recipe as treadcore-client's metal.rs;
  • metal keys with lamps and digits;
  • black readout wells for the content.

All five views share one header. The look is the default; the flat palettes remain behind --light and --dark. The story of the views, and crucible as grove's second job, are the next steps.

III. Methods

  1. draw.rs:
    • Theme.metal and Theme.engrave;
    • METAL;
    • Finish (Plate, Button, Pressed), finish_pixels, the cache;
    • Canvas::metal and Canvas::well.
  2. ui.rs: in metal, panel draws a plate, button a capsule (pressed while held), and rail_item a key; well is new.
  3. nav.rs: header (frame, name, keys, status strip, body well), width_for, HEADER_H 40.
  4. The views: Training, Machines, Sessions and Media use nav::header. Lab's header, rail, bar and status are drawn on the steel. Training's pages are keys.
  5. main.rs: --light; METAL otherwise.
  6. Checks: cargo test (344 pass); frames of every view; installed and relaunched; memory read twice, 20 s apart.

IV. Results

Errors.

  • The first build left the strip's width computed before the theme was known, and Lab's fleet name drew over the Sessions key. Fixed: width_for(theme).
  • Plates drawn twice made a seam in Lab's rail. Fixed: the rail is the frame's own steel.
  • Sessions' tile titles were pale on steel. Fixed: engraved.

V. Discussion

The look carries over without touching what each page says. The pages already drew their rows as tiles on a base, and those became readouts on steel. What the step does not yet do is the story: the views are still in the order they were built.
