Abstract

Auto mode promoted its first candidate. grove/w0034 passed the brain's 109 tests and all nine families, and is main as dc3df01, its seven defaults built next on every machine. The pass that pushed it failed just after. It left an unsaved state and two runs, which were fixed and made impossible. A rotation now asks one run of each kind in turn, in two lanes, with fresh seeds. The Runs page describes each button as it works in the cycle.

V. Discussion

The bar held, and the promotion is a large one: seven defaults, two of them doubled. That is what the search had been pointing at since LR 116, and the first promotion stopped at the test LR 117 made into a scalar.

The failed pass is the cost of a planner that pushes. Its push could not be undone by the failure, and its state was never saved, and it left runs a correct pass would not have. Both are fixed. The rule from it: a pass writes nothing outside its state until it keeps it, the push being the one deliberate exception.
