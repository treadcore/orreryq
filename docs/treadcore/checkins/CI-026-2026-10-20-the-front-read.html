Abstract

Four backlog items, in order. The team board's front, built every second since LR 24 and read by nothing, now leans the Expand rung's choice of depot (w_front, 0.5): a depot's cost is half its distance to the base and half its distance to the front, and the pick is kept until taken. The meter showed the front known at only 2.5 of Expand's picks a match, since the board counts only hostiles in sight, so the board now remembers hostile tanks for front_memory (30) seconds, for the front alone: 3.2 picks a match with a front, 14% without. A scenario holds with the part and fails at 0; the guard census reads -0.14 +/- 0.42 a seat over 48 seeds. Each scenario family now carries a floor, and make families runs all five at 1,000 seeds in 31 s; each falls under its floor with its part broken. Allies' goals on the coordination screen were found already shown by a hosting window and are now proven by a test; a joining window needs the wire. The crews' hand-out and the hop's bridge choice have tests of their own, and 22 functions got doc comments (121 undocumented to 99).

Index terms: team board, influence front, sighting memory, guard census, scenario floors, fleet command.

V. Discussion

  1. A front of what is in sight is a flicker. The board reads one member's observation a second; LR 35 found it for the landfall, and the front needed the same memory.
  2. Count before you census. Expand picks about four depots a match. The meter said in the first minutes of a 45-minute census that the part barely acted.
  3. Floors make families a regression suite. Five families in 31 s now stand guard over the reflexes the census cannot resolve.
