Abstract

A second machine, columbia (192.168.8.224, Intel i3-N300, 8 threads, Copal on Alpine 3.24.3), played one job of the census machine's run with the run's own frozen binary, at the run's commit, and its log matched the census machine's line for line apart from the two timing figures. It can play censuses beside saskatchewan's.
