Abstract

The night's SPSA point passed its held-out rule by 0.002 of an error. The rule's second guard, make families, passed: all nine hold, the duel 5 points lower, inside the noise. Because the pass was so narrow and the point lay far outside the range the screen read, one more census read it on 400 fresh seeds, its rule written first: +0.09 +/- 0.27, inside the noise. The point is not promoted; no default changes. The second read caught what the first held-out read let through.

V. Discussion

The rule as written would have promoted a point worth nothing. A single held-out read at two errors is right about 97 times in 100 when there is nothing to find, but SPSA points are chosen for reading high, and the bar meets that bias at its weakest. Two proposals for Paul (LR 88): promote an SPSA point only after two held-out reads, or one of 800 seeds; and bound SPSA to the range the screen has read.
