Abstract

Three landfall parts (CI-008 to CI-010) each assumed where the gun that sinks a boat stands, and none moved the deaths. This step measured it, with no change to play. Over 164 landfall deaths in 16 seeds, the killer stood a median 8 tiles from the boat, inside its 10-tile direct range and at the edge of the 10-tile sight. 38% stood within reach of the boat's chosen beach, a median 20 tiles off and out of sight; 60% stood nearer the boat than its beach, along the run; 9% were afloat. A boat sees its killer only once the killer can fire. CI-009's "boats are not blind" is withdrawn. The knowledge the team does not use is the team board's hostile influence, which backlog 35 proposes to price.

Index terms: partial observability, sensing range, team knowledge, measurement.

V. Discussion

  1. A sighting is worth something only from beyond the gun's range. With sight equal to range, a boat's own eyes never give warning, so no rule over what it sees can save it.
  2. The team already builds the knowledge. The board's hostile influence spreads each seen tank's pull over sectors and keeps it. It has been built and unread since CI-001. The landfall is the first decision a death count says it should feed.
