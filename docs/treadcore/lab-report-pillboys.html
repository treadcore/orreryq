Lab report: the Pillboy requirement, checked against the prior art

12 October 2026, evening. A requirement arrived in the form of two documents: a beginner's tutorial for a "Tank Pillboy Game" (a prior-art descendant with pillboxes, depots, momentum driving and boats), and a technical audit of that tutorial against the prior art's original 1980s manual. The audit's verdict: the tutorial matched the prior art's core loop but got three things wrong, and could not, from a manual alone, say anything about the game's code. This report takes the audit's corrections as the requirement for treadcore's Pillboys, checks each against what treadcore already says and does, records what was decided, and says what is and is not done.

1. The requirement, as received

The audit corrected the tutorial on these points:

  1. Construction is builder-mediated. In the prior art a click does not make a pillbox. It orders a builder, who leaves the tank, travels to the square, does the work if the material is there, and returns. The builder can be killed while out.
  2. Refuelling bases are not pillboxes. Bases replenish shells, mines and armor when the tank drives onto them, and are captured by being driven onto. There is no "repair the base" action; the tutorial had invented one by confusing base maintenance with pillbox repair.
  3. Range control is a cursor, not a weapon setting. The player can move an aim cursor nearer or farther; most shots do not need it because a shell hits the first obstacle; its real use is detonating mines at a distance.
  4. Movement has momentum and terrain-limited speed. Acceleration and deceleration take time; swamp and water are slow, roads and boats fast; a tank can overshoot a road into swamp.
  5. Driving is keyboard; clicks are for the builder. The tutorial's "click anywhere to control the tank" was wrong for the prior art.
  6. A manual cannot verify code. Exact update order, whether terrain limits velocity or applies drag, and the boat-boarding rule are unknowable without the source.

2. Method

Each point was compared with three things: the brief as recorded (CONCEPT.md sections 1 and 7), the design (DESIGN.md), and the code as it ran this evening (source/treadcore-sim/src/lib.rs, map.rs, rules.rs; the trace from make debug). Where treadcore had no answer, one was decided and written into DESIGN.md before any code, which is what ROADMAP.md step 3 requires.

4. What was done

  • DESIGN.md gained "Pillboys and pillboxes: the decision": the control model, the squad's data (strength, aboard, pos, task, progress, material), the four tasks, what a deployed squad risks, what depots remain, what pillboxes are (the prior art's, plus a short-range gun against drones), and what this must not change.
  • ROADMAP.md step 3 now points at that decision and at this report.
  • Nothing in treadcore-sim was written for Pillboys. The roadmap orders the replay (step 1) and the world hash (step 2) first, so the change to the step function that Pillboys bring can be shown to do only what it meant to.

5. Conclusion

The requirement is accepted, with the three corrections the audit made taken as given: Pillboys are the prior art's builder (an order, resolved over time, under fire, by a squad that can be lost); depots stay refuelling bases with no repair action; aiming is a coordinate, which is the range cursor made general. Two divergences from the prior art are deliberate and are now written down: the player orders a destination rather than steering, and shells fly over what is between the gun and the aim point because indirect fire is the point of the game.

The audit's last point is the one worth keeping in mind. It could not tell, from the prior art's manual, how the prior art's movement really worked. treadcore can answer the same questions about itself in one file of constants and one step function, and a trace that shows them running: terrain limits the maximum speed (TANK_SPEED / move_cost), the update order is orders, tanks, drones, shells, depots, swarm, match, and there are no boats. When the Pillboys are built, the same will be true of them.

6. Not done

The Pillboy, pillbox and build code: roadmap step 3, after steps 1 and 2. A playable test of the decision (does ordering a squad feel like commanding, or like waiting?) needs the window, which this VM cannot yet open (BUILD.md, "The window in this VM").
