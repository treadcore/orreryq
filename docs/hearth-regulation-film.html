A fleet heats an apartment

Narration
A fleet of eight computers heats an apartment whose own heating is switched off. Every five minutes Orrery reads two thermometers in the room and the weather at the airport, works out how many watts the room wants moved, and takes one step: a lane of work paused or resumed, or a machine powered off and later woken.

80 °F, and under 81 °F

Narration
Since 16:44 on the seventeenth of November it holds that room at eighty degrees Fahrenheit, by itself. The ceiling is eighty-one. In the first fifteen hours the room went to about eighty-one point six, once, and a person had to end it. How is it used? What is it calculating? Did it hold the room? What does it need from a person?

Part one: how to use it

Part one. How to use it.

Where the panels are

Narration
In Orrery: Queue, then Heat. The Heat page has six panels. The first two are for this. Hearth is the controls. Diagram is the picture: where the heat comes from, where it goes, and the thermometers.

Setting the temperature

Narration
To set the temperature, press minus or plus zero point five, or type a number and press Return. Too warm right now? Lower the target by a degree. A door opened is faster.

The three modes

Narration
Three modes. Off: nothing is read and nothing is done. Advise: every five minutes it says what it would do, and changes no machine. Act: it does it. The button is framed in red and must be pressed twice. To take over, press advise. Lanes it paused stay paused.

The power row

Narration
The power row has a cell for each machine, and one button. On sends the wake packet. Off, pressed twice: the machine finishes its job, leaves the queue, and is powered off. The machine that carries the thermometers has no Off. The controller powers off only a machine known to come back. Three are proved.

The Hearth panel, at 08:19 on the eighteenth, in act. One: the target, in both scales. Two: changing it. Three: the mode. Four: the power switch. It has no button; it is set from the command line. Five: the hold: the law in force, its budget in watts, and its last words.

The Hearth panel, marks 6 to 10

Narration

Green is the watts drawn now; the mark is the watts wanted. Ten: the ladder, the older law. It does not act while the law is hold. The red paragraph says what act will do. Read it before pressing act.

Part two: the science

Narration
Part two. The science.

One balance

Narration
The room is treated as one lump of heat. C times the room's slope equals the fleet's watts, plus every other watt, less what the walls lose. The room and the weather are read. So are the fleet's watts, with a model where a machine has no counter. C is measured: four point two megajoules a degree. U A, the watts lost for each degree over the weather, is not measured. One hundred and twenty is the figure in use. P nought, every watt that is not the fleet's, is not measured either. It is what is left over.

Three things follow

Narration
Three things follow. Where the room settles: the weather, plus all the watts over U A. How fast: C over U A. Nine point seven hours, if U A is one hundred and twenty. And the three flows on the Diagram. Through the walls: U A times the gap to the weather. Soak: C times the slope. Other heat: walls plus soak, less the fleet. It is as wrong as U A is.

The budget

Narration
The controller does not use gains. It asks how many watts must be added or taken away for the room to stop where it is wanted. The first term is what the walls will lose once the room is at the target. The second is what the room is storing as it moves now. It is held within six hundred watts. Then one step. Graphics machines are kept, and the machine that spends most energy on a job goes first. Forty minutes between changes, because the room answers about an hour late.

Calculation 1: a budget on a quiet morning

Narration
A budget, on a quiet morning. The room at twenty-six point zero, the target twenty-six point seven, falling seven hundredths of a degree an hour. The U A term: minus eighty-four watts. The C term: minus eighty-two. The budget: plus one hundred and sixty-six watts. Every machine was already on, so nothing was done.

Calculation 2: a budget at its limit

Narration
A budget at its limit. The room at twenty-seven point one, rising zero point six nine degrees an hour. The U A term is only forty-eight watts. The C term is eight hundred and five. Minus eight hundred and fifty-three, held at minus six hundred. Not far over its target, but rising fast. A law that looked only at the error would have done almost nothing.

Calculation 3: the three flows at 08:21

Narration
The three flows on the Diagram, at 08:21. Through the walls: one hundred and twenty times fifteen point eight. Eighteen hundred and ninety-six watts. Soak: seven hundred watts. Other heat: thirteen hundred and eighty-seven watts. It is implied, and it rests on a U A that is not measured.

Calculation 5: a night with every machine on

Narration
A night with every machine on. From two to seven the room stood at twenty-six point zero, the weather averaged five point seven, the fleet twelve hundred and eighty watts. So all the watts over U A are twenty point three degrees. If the other heat were nothing, U A would be sixty-three. That is a floor. If U A is one hundred and twenty, the other heat is eleven hundred and fifty-six watts. One steady night gives one equation in two unknowns.

Calculation 6: how cold it can be

Narration
How cold can it be? The fleet's most is about thirteen hundred watts. The coldest weather at which eighty can be held is about six degrees Celsius, whatever the other heat. That night it went to three, and the room stood under its target with every machine working.

Part three: the first fifteen hours

Narration
Part three. The first fifteen hours.

The record

Narration
Orrery took over at 16:44. Until 18:20 it held on target. Then the room rose zero point six degrees an hour. The law shed one hundred and forty-three watts, then seventy-three. A person shed the rest. The peak was about eighty-one point six. It fell back under the ceiling by 19:36. Overnight heat was added a step at a time, and the weather fell faster. From two to seven every machine was on, and the room stood under its target. Then the morning warmed. Seventy-eight point one to eighty-one point three by the controller's own reading.

Eight faults, seven corrected

Narration
Eight faults were found by watching it run. One probe jumps a degree by itself, and a machine was switched off for it. A switch-off did not count as a change until the machine was down. Only named spares could be woken. Asked for six hundred watts, it shed one hundred and forty-three. At the ceiling, a spiking probe was not believed. The machine with the thermometers was paused. A large budget reached past small steps. Those seven were corrected the same day. The eighth is open: it should act on where the room is heading.

Part four: how the heat is drawn

Narration
Part four. How the heat is drawn.

Nesting, colour, width, movement, a tank

Narration
The Diagram is a process diagram, of the kind a control room uses. Nesting: the weather encloses the walls, the walls the apartment. Heat can only go outward. One colour, one meaning: yellow the fleet, orange the room, blue the weather, violet the other heat. Width is watts: a pixel for each seventy. Movement is direction. And a tank for what is stored, the term people forget.

Hatching, dashes, bubbles, words, thermometers

Narration
Hatching for the walls, dashes for what is not measured. Instrument bubbles: a ring is a thing that is read. The two numbers that rest on a guess say so, in the warning colour. Thermometers drawn as thermometers, on one scale. Trends beside the numbers, and clocks on every machine: three time scales are why the controller waits.

Part five: upkeep

Narration
Part five. Upkeep.

Daily and weekly

Narration
Daily, look at the hold line: its plan under fifteen minutes old, its last words making sense. Weekly, save the weather and read the house's fit. A week missed is a week lost. After a power cut there is nothing to set. When a machine is added, prove it wakes.

When something is wrong

Narration
When something is wrong. The plan is twenty minutes old: is the keeper running? No room probe is fresh: restart radbeeper on saskatchewan. Over eighty-one and rising: lower the target, open a door, press Off on a Supermicro. Too cold and nothing left: every machine is on. Nothing to do. Jobs sent back every few minutes: close the app. The cause is not found.

Done, and not done

Narration
What is done, and what is not. The hold runs on the live fleet, in act, and held the room with one excursion. Seven of eight faults are corrected. Three machines are proved to wake. Not done. U A is not measured, and so neither is the other heat. Zone two has no thermometer. The eighth fault is open. The hold has no proof of its own: two days of running. And On and Off have not been pressed in the app.

The close, and the credits

Narration
The fleet holds the room at eighty degrees. The house's loss per degree is still not measured, and everything that rests on it says so.

How the film is made

The film is one file of words and numbers (storyboard.json) drawn by three scripts: tools/report/film.py, what any such film shares; tools/report/process_film.py, whose look, title, close, teleprompt and kit this film keeps; and tools/report/hearth-regulation_film.py, the kinds of slide.

The words first
Each slide is exactly as long as its narration at 150 words a minute, with a breath before and after. A mark in the narration is a cue, and what appears on a slide appears at a cue.
A slideshow
The report is a guide, then a science, then a record. It has no single block diagram to walk, so this film has no map and no camera: one idea a slide.
The pictures
The report's two pictures of Orrery are shown whole, five times between them, and what the narration names is ringed as it is named, with its mark's number. The rings are regions measured on the pictures for this film; the report's own marks are numbers at a point. The Diagram is shown twice more for the ways the heat is drawn, and the chart of the report before this one once.
The calculations
Five of the report's seven, with its numbers: the budget at 06:37, the budget held at −600 W at 18:37, the three flows at 08:21, the night that gives (P + P0)/UA = 20.3 °C, and how cold it can be. The time constant is on the slide before them. The energy of calculation 7 is not in the film.
Not done
There is no still of each slide on this page. The film has no sound.
